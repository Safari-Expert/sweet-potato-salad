# Activities, transfers and flights

## Activities

1. A master is the resolved place of performance and the actual activity name. Use the connected customer's canonical location. Check duration in minutes (1.5 hours = 90), capacity, safety rules, minimum party, inclusions and service level; distinguish town, park and gateway. Keep descriptions accurate, under 381 characters, with any essential guardian/height restriction. Mark an authorized estimated duration as estimated.
2. `group_flg=FALSE` represents the cost for one eligible participant even when price changes by group size. `TRUE` represents the entire priced group. Build disjoint age × pax × validity bands, only for allowed combinations. Do not multiply a per-person tariff by pax count or divide a private group charge. `max_cap` covers `max_pax`. Share stable master details/currency across rows. Do not merge products with distinct location, capacity or charging basis merely because their copy resembles each other.
3. Intersect each child fare with the activity's admission minimum. A boat trip for ages eight and up cannot acquire a free age-zero row from a general infant policy. Use explicit child/teen rates where available, calculating actual payable amounts against the stated adult basis. A general family rule is usable only when its scope and ages are established. Retain independent size and age discounts. Separate optional services from included transport/meals and park fees only when evidence supports the split. A missing price remains unresolved. A descriptive zero-rate product belongs only to an explicitly requested descriptive-only task.

## Transfers

Establish the commercial unit: whole vehicle per route/day, shared per seat, or flat meet-and-assist. Retain a stable vehicle/service master for each materially distinct service level and capacity. Pax tiers with changing price for one vehicle stay under one master. Match the customer’s vehicle and real source/destination names; never infer a nearby gateway. Daily hire uses `transfer_type=daily` and both endpoints blank. Point-to-point uses `taxi` and both resolved endpoints. Shared per-seat tariffs require a supported model; the current template validator accepts daily/taxi. Do not silently relabel a bus. A per-seat authorized representation has one seat per priced unit, transport capacity retained separately.

Include A→B and B→A for each relevant service variant unless explicitly one-way; use each direction’s published price if they differ. Emit one A→A rule per band for a genuine meet-and-assist at the same airport. Keep regular/VIP products distinct, checking directions for each. Whole-vehicle cost is the full amount; preserve seasonal and disjoint pax tiers. Use ages 0–99 without discounts under the transfer convention unless the source calls for a different supported decision. Keep actual `max_cap` consistent and at least the largest `max_pax`. A real zero minimum can be retained. Check all service × route × tier × validity combinations, with closures documented. Avoid inventing an airport endpoint for daily hire.

## Flights

Use one stable airline master and currency, one row per actual directional sector, scheduled departure/arrival, age and validity. Resolve airstrips or approved endpoint mappings. Confirm operating days, dates, fare class, net/STO basis, included surcharges, inducement, aircraft constraints and source amendments. Preserve a published NET already including fuel unchanged. A network map does not create all pairs; only create return sectors with supporting fare and schedule. Keep package and one-way fare conditions separate.

Record weekday digits from Monday 1 to Sunday 7, unique and comma-separated, e.g. `1,3,5`. Quote this CSV field. Use consistent 24-hour `HH:MM` local clock times. Flag overnight/timezone offsets the template cannot express. Distinct weekdays alone do not distinguish overlapping rows in the validator’s airline + endpoints + departure + arrival grouping. Resolve conflicts without changing times to get a pass. Enforce seasonal closures and actual inclusive age bands; a free infant row or discounted child needs evidence. Every modeled fare has `min_pax=1` and `max_pax=99`, including free fares, while supplier physical capacity/minimum seats stay in the evidence. Do not multiply per-person fare by a seat guarantee.

Exclude Dynamic fares by default; keep the reason and count separately from blockers. An actual fixed rate for a defined seasonal window is usable only within that window. Compare the complete schedule, weekday list, age/date/pax, amounts and read-back IDs. Attach each actual supplier rate PDF and relevant separately issued amendment to the airline, verifying the stored names and preserving existing files.
