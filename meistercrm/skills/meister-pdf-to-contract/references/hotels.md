## Room shares, capacity and age

Normalize room totals to per-person sharing cost using the priced occupancy; per-person quotes already have that unit. Reconstruct whole rooms, single guests, full occupancy, a mixed party, and all supported boards from the emitted values. A quoted Single at capacity one takes its full quoted price and zero Single supplement. For paying rows in Double or larger products, `single_supplement` equals the matched adult's payable `cost` in the same room, season and compulsory-meal scope, including on discounted paid child rows. Free rows have zero applicable supplements, with unavailable boards blank. A free infant has no independent Single entitlement. Check partial occupancy explicitly: a single missing-place amount may not recreate every board-specific full-room total. Record any discrepancy and resolve it within that scope.

An extra bed with a real supported adult price is a distinct product. Keep the standard room and, e.g., `Double Room + Extra Bed`. Sum the two-person room and extra charge then divide by three; normalize each actual place only once. Per-person meal increments remain per person. Use ` + Extra Guest` when the extra place is a guest rather than a bed. A quoted Triple is independent. A child-only optional third bed with no adult triple quote stays a capacity-two room with the child-only option recorded for manual handling. Do not infer maximum occupancy, age, or a commercial product from a floor plan. A flat unit or first-adult-plus-extra tariff needs party-total checks before any averaging. Preserve source minima and guest rules.

Read admission first, then sleeping price and child meal terms. Enforce adult/child composition, sharing/own room and age exemptions. A specifically priced lower endpoint can establish admission despite generic wording at that boundary; explicit exclusions still win. A single missing age between adjacent ranges goes to the younger bracket. Where both priced bands claim the same endpoint use the higher applicable cost within the same scope. Record each convention. When older bands clearly imply an eligible youngest unpaid interval, apply the free-infant convention including available meals; do not invent admission for an excluded age. Unqualified per-person rates can apply to eligible paying children when no discount is given.

“Pays 25%” uses 0.25 times the named base; “25% off” uses 0.75. A bare percentage below 50% is treated as a discount; exactly 50% is half; a bare percentage above 50% needs context or a human decision. Distinguish extra-adult, adult sharing, room-total and board bases. Derive the child's room and meal bands independently, including teens' non-alcoholic packages. Where the tariff differs by adult count, use the child-sharing-with-one-adult rule for the general row and audit parties with more adults. When a positional child composition cannot be encoded in one rate and the approved fallback applies, retain the matched adult accommodation cost with independently supported child meal prices; record the lost distinction. Own-room child percentages or limited complimentary child places cannot be applied to every child indiscriminately. Preserve explicitly charged child extras in evidence, including the difference when a free-row encoding cannot express them.

## Meals and one-time adjustments

`cost` is the sleeping anchor labelled BB in the catalog, even when the supplier only sells a more inclusive product. Never invent a cheaper breakfast or room-only price. Each board upgrade independently adds to `cost`. Choose matched package differences before separate à-la-carte menus, on the same unit, occupancy, guest, season, VAT and currency basis. If two amounts are room totals, subtract and divide by occupancy once. Blank means unavailable in the output. Zero means included/available. Unquoted options need evidence, never a fabricated zero.

HB normally means breakfast and dinner; FB adds lunch. Use a documented lunch alternative only when selected. “Meals included” in the plural without a limiting list implies FB: retain inclusive cost, FB zero, HB and AI blank unless independently offered. AI-only properties retain the full supported package in `cost` with HB and FB blank and AI zero. A game package is not automatically a board plan. Calculate child discounts on the components the supplier actually discounts. A free room does not independently establish free supplier meals.

Split compulsory charges by actual dates and eligible guests. Add compulsory meals to cost once, removing their included value from board upgrades. For accommodation share B, ordinary lunch L, dinner D and compulsory amounts already included ML and MD: cost = B + ML + MD, HB = D − MD, FB = L + D − ML − MD. A replacement festive dinner replaces ordinary dinner; an incremental festive surcharge adds only its increment. An optional meal or printed menu is not compulsory without terms. Keep board increments independent and recompute the adult-matched single supplement after compulsory changes.

## VAT, country adjustments and rounding

Select the published VAT-inclusive buying table when both inclusive and exclusive prices cover the same property, product, board, dates, unit and agreement. Apply the STO/NET eligibility rules first. When only the exclusive table is available, keep its quoted prices. Never add or deduct VAT, gross up or net down. Keep a supplied hotel `tax` column at numeric zero. Preserve the selected template if it omits the column. A VAT-only ambiguity does not block conversion.

Resolve the hotel’s actual jurisdiction and any applicable country policy. For Tanzania, use [VAT and TDL](../country/tanzania/vat-and-tdl.md) for the mainland, Unguja and Pemba. For other countries, keep the supplier’s wording and use only a separately confirmed policy for that jurisdiction. Record a missing decision when it affects payable prices.

Keep full decimal precision through room allocations, board differences and applicable country adjustments. Round final hotel money once to two decimal places with `ROUND_HALF_UP`. Keep the original quoted values and units, unadjusted model, adjustment basis and final amounts on `Original`. Preserve numeric zero displayed as `0`, empty unavailable board options, numeric dates displayed as `yyyy-mm-dd`, and the agreed literal uppercase hotel flags.

## Booking limits and difficult party totals

For ordinary hotel rooms default to min_pax=1 and max_pax=max_cap, applying any explicit booking minimum. A newly created extra-bed variant sells at supported full occupancy (min_pax=max_pax=max_cap). A single-use quote for a Double does not create a Single + Extra Bed. A Honeymoon Suite with no other capacity evidence uses one to two pax and capacity two; label this as an assumption.

A family/group-oriented unit with an explicit booking minimum m and no maximum uses m + 2 for maximum pax and capacity, unless an explicit maximum, connected component rooms, adult ceiling or child-only restriction controls. Record this as an assumption; a charge for minimum occupancy is not automatically an admission minimum. An interconnected unit with two confirmed two-person rooms supports four places. The divisor for a fixed unit quote is the number of *priced* places, independent of physical capacity. A free child need not reduce an exclusive-use price. A villa's bedroom count cannot be inferred from total pax. If no capacity clues exist, hold that rate.

When a quoted Single special applies only to the first few rooms in a booking, encode the quoted Single and record the actual limit, excess tariff and manual booking action on the master. Rates cannot automatically enforce the counter across rooms. For a known per-person extra without child-specific terms, use the full fee for paying children and zero for free infants under the preparation convention; evidence specifying an all-guests charge, discount or exception takes precedence. Check adult-only, child-only, shared, minimum and partially occupied groups and retain any accepted discrepancy in the evidence.

When a high/low meal table permits a cheaper HB product under an FB quote, use the supported cheaper baseline and positive FB difference only when the amounts and units are established. A replacement for one holiday meal does not substitute automatically for a missing second meal. For each gala date start from that date's own room and age rate. Keep separate the room validity, event price, single compulsory meal, per-night supplement, minimum stay, arrival and named-holiday attendance restrictions. In two-night offers, apply a second-night discount only to that eligible night.
