# MeisterCRM

Connect your MeisterCRM account to work with travel leads, tasks, proposals, and catalog records. Meister Operator guides you through the available CRM actions and approvals. Meister PDF to Contract converts supplier documents into reviewable rates. Meister Contract Operator checks those rates against your catalog, imports approved changes and verifies the saved result.

After installation, connect MeisterCRM, sign in, and choose the workspace actions to approve. Start by asking which workspace you are connected to. The approved actions and your current CRM permissions determine what you can do. Manage or disconnect the connection under **Settings → Integrations → MCP** in MeisterCRM.
